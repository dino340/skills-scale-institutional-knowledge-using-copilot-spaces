# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Engineering Managers

### Role Summary
Engineering Managers help the delivery team operate effectively by aligning technical execution with project priorities, team capacity, and delivery health. They serve as a bridge between product goals, engineering work, and day-to-day team execution.

### Responsibilities
- Manage engineering capacity, priorities, and team planning
- Partner with Product Managers and Project Managers to sequence work and mitigate bottlenecks
- Support developers with technical planning, dependency management, and execution risks
- Help maintain delivery quality, velocity, and team sustainability
- Facilitate engineering-level communication around trade-offs, resourcing, and milestones

### How they interact with existing roles
- With Product Managers: translate roadmap priorities into executable engineering plans and communicate trade-offs.
- With Project Managers: align on schedule, dependencies, milestones, and delivery risk.
- With Developers: support planning, technical decision-making, and day-to-day execution without replacing individual ownership.

### Goals
- Improve team delivery predictability and engineering health
- Reduce blockers and coordination overhead
- Maintain a sustainable pace while meeting project goals

### Typical Communication
- Engineering planning and team capacity discussions
- Cross-functional delivery reviews
- Dependency and risk updates with PMs and Product leads

---

## QA / Test Leads

### Role Summary
QA / Test Leads define the testing approach, quality gates, and validation strategy for releases. They ensure the product meets acceptance criteria and can be shipped with confidence.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy, coverage plans, and acceptance validation
- Partner with developers to review quality risks and testability
- Coordinate regression, smoke, and release validation activities
- Identify defects early and support remediation follow-through
- Help confirm when work is ready for stakeholder review or release

### How they interact with existing roles
- With Developers: review testability, triage issues, and confirm defect readiness.
- With Project Managers: provide status on quality readiness, release risk, and validation coverage.
- With Product Managers: validate that delivered work meets acceptance criteria and customer needs.

### Goals
- Reduce release risk and defect leakage
- Improve confidence in product quality and user experience
- Align validation work with delivery milestones and readiness criteria

### Typical Communication
- Test plans and quality status reviews
- Release readiness and defect triage discussions
- Acceptance validation with Product and stakeholder groups

---

## Release Managers / Deployment Leads

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate release readiness, deployment sequencing, and post-deployment validation. They help reduce operational risk and ensure changes reach production in a controlled and observable way.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate deployment windows, release checklists, and rollback plans
- Validate readiness across engineering, QA, and stakeholder teams
- Monitor production release health and support post-deploy verification
- Communicate release status, known issues, and mitigation steps
- Partner with support and leadership on launch communications

### How they interact with existing roles
- With Project Managers: align release timing, dependencies, and communications.
- With Developers and QA: ensure readiness, rollback planning, and verification steps are complete.
- With Product Managers and stakeholders: communicate changes, impact, and deployment status.

### Goals
- Minimize release risk and production disruption
- Improve operational readiness and release consistency
- Support reliable delivery of changes to customers

### Typical Communication
- Release readiness reviews and deployment approvals
- Change and incident communications
- Post-release verification and issue triage

---

## Security / Compliance Leads

### Role Summary
Security / Compliance Leads ensure security, privacy, and governance requirements are considered throughout the project lifecycle. They help teams identify and mitigate risks before they become blockers or incidents.

### Responsibilities
- Review project work against security, privacy, and compliance standards
- Identify risk areas and recommend mitigations or controls
- Coordinate security reviews, audits, and required sign-offs
- Partner with engineering and PMs on risk treatment and escalations
- Ensure project documentation reflects required controls and responsibilities

### How they interact with existing roles
- With Engineering Managers and Developers: review technical design, risk areas, and implementation safeguards.
- With Project Managers: help assess risk, required approvals, and timeline impact.
- With Product Managers and stakeholders: align security requirements with delivery goals and timelines.

### Goals
- Reduce security and compliance risk
- Support trusted, sustainable product delivery
- Ensure governance needs are integrated early and consistently

### Typical Communication
- Security review and risk assessment meetings
- Compliance checkpoints and approval discussions
- Escalation and mitigation planning with project leadership

---

## Customer Success / Stakeholder Liaison

### Role Summary
Customer Success or Stakeholder Liaisons ensure that customer and stakeholder expectations remain visible throughout delivery. They help translate project progress into stakeholder understanding and feedback.

### Responsibilities
- Track stakeholder needs, feedback, and expected outcomes
- Communicate delivery progress, timelines, and risks to relevant groups
- Gather customer or stakeholder input and share it with Product and Project teams
- Support adoption readiness and change communication planning
- Help identify gaps between delivery progress and stakeholder expectations

### How they interact with existing roles
- With Product Managers: share customer insights, feedback, and market context.
- With Project Managers: align on stakeholder updates, risks, and communication plans.
- With Developers and QA: help clarify expected outcomes and validate readiness from a user perspective.

### Goals
- Strengthen stakeholder alignment and trust
- Improve feedback loops during delivery
- Support a smoother transition from project execution to adoption

### Typical Communication
- Stakeholder updates and milestone reviews
- Customer feedback summaries and adoption readiness check-ins
- Escalation and expectation-setting discussions

- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- The full set of personas helps teams understand ownership, handoffs, and cross-functional accountability across the project lifecycle.
